About Euphonia

An AI-powered music platform built with modern web technologies, generative intelligence, and high-performance audio streaming.

Gemini AI Curation

Harnesses Google's generative models to curate dynamic 5-track playlists from abstract moods, genres, and languages in milliseconds.

Live Global Search

Integrates direct metadata resolvers to browse millions of tracks, complete with high-resolution artwork and instant audio previews.

Secure Authentication

Combines official Google Identity Services OAuth 2.0 with cryptographic backend token verification and SQLite persistence.

Custom Audio Engine

Features zero-dependency SVG transport controls, interactive scrubbers, volume controls, and real-time AI lyrics synchronization.
